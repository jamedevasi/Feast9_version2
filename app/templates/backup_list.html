Excel Copy (Plan B)

{{ icon('download') }} Download Excel Copy

Everything in one spreadsheet, to keep the clinic running if Feast9 is unavailable: upcoming appointments, patients with their allergies and medical conditions, cases with balances and follow-ups, payments, visit notes, prescriptions and lab work.

While Feast9 is down, add new patients and cases as new rows, and raise a case's Paid amount when a payment comes in. When Feast9 is back, import the file below. Only what you added or changed is brought in.

This file contains every patient's health and payment details. Keep it on a secure computer, don't email or share it, and delete old copies. Download a fresh copy regularly, for example at the end of each day.

Import from Excel

{{ icon('download') }} Download Template

Bring in patients, cases and payments from a spreadsheet: an Excel copy you kept working in, records from the earlier version of Feast9, or paper records typed into the template.

  • From the earlier version: the template's first columns (A to V) match its Excel export exactly. Copy them from its "Patients & Cases" sheet and paste into the template. Then fill in the highlighted columns.
  • Privacy Notice Accepted is recorded as you enter it: Yes for patients who have accepted the data notice. A patient with No, or left blank, is still imported and is marked in Feast9 as not yet accepted, until you record it on their page.
  • Red columns must be filled on every row: Patient Name and Sex.
  • Amber columns are needed on some rows: Doctor when the row has a case, and Guardian Name and Mobile for a patient under 18.
  • Each row can hold one case. A patient with several cases has several rows, and they're kept as one patient.
